## Page 14 — The Process by Which Physics Developed

1. In every era, people believe they understand nature well.
2. Clear evidence appears that the then-prevailing understanding of nature cannot explain something.
3. The reason why that evidence could not be explained is found.
4. The newly found principle not only resolves the previously unexplained problem but also reveals entirely new additional truths.
5. The newly discovered truth makes it possible to find other evidence that had previously been unknowable.

## Page 61 — Slide 62 — Law of Velocity Addition Under the Galilean Transformation

Starting from the Galilean transformation:

$$x' = x - vt, \quad t' = t$$

$$\implies \frac{x'}{t'} = \frac{x - vt}{t} = \frac{x}{t} - v$$

Defining:

$$v_{CA} = \frac{x}{t}, \quad v_{CB} = \frac{x'}{t'}, \quad v_{BA} = v$$

$$\implies v_{CB} = v_{CA} - v_{BA}$$

$$\implies \boxed{v_{CA} = v_{CB} + v_{BA}}$$

**Law of Velocity Addition**

## Page 67 — Slide 68 — Length Contraction in Special Relativity

**A moving rod is shortened in the direction of motion.**

## Page 68 — Slide 69 — Galilean Transformation → Lorentz Transformation

*(Diagram: same rod-in-two-frames diagram as before)*

Length of rod in frame B: $x' - 0 = x'$

Length of rod in frame A: $x - vt$

Since length contraction requires $x - vt = x'/\gamma$:

**Galilean transformation:**
$$x' = x - vt, \quad t' = t$$

**Lorentz transformation:**
$$\boxed{x' = \gamma(x - \beta ct), \quad ct' = \gamma(ct - \beta x)}$$

$$\beta = \frac{v}{c}$$

*(Section marker: 01E)*

---

## Page 69 — Slide 70 — Relativistic Law of Velocity Addition from the Lorentz Transformation

Starting from the Lorentz transformation:

$$x' = \gamma(x - \beta ct), \quad ct' = \gamma(ct - \beta x)$$

$$\implies \frac{x'}{t'} = \frac{x - vt}{t - \dfrac{vx}{c^2}} = \frac{\dfrac{x}{t} - v}{1 - \dfrac{x}{t}\cdot\dfrac{v}{c^2}}$$

With $v_{CA} = x/t$, $v_{CB} = x'/t'$, $v_{BA} = v$:

$$v_{CB} = \frac{v_{CA} - v_{BA}}{1 - \dfrac{v_{CA}\,v_{BA}}{c^2}}$$

$$\implies \boxed{v_{CA} = \frac{v_{CB} + v_{BA}}{1 + \dfrac{v_{CB}\,v_{BA}}{c^2}}}$$

**New law of velocity addition**

*(Section marker: 01E)*

---

## Page 70 — Slide 71 — Relativistic Velocity Addition Resolves the Constancy of the Speed of Light

$$v_{CA} = \frac{v_{CB} + v_{BA}}{1 + \dfrac{v_{CB}\,v_{BA}}{c^2}}$$

With $v_{CB} = c$ and $v_{BA} = 0.3c$:

$$v_{CA} = \frac{c + 0.3c}{1 + 0.3} = c$$

**The problem of the constancy of the speed of light is thus resolved!!**

*(Section marker: 01E)*

## Page 73 — Slide 74 — Einstein's Special Theory of Relativity (Germany, 1879–1955)

*(Diagram: photograph of Einstein (left) and Nobel Prize medal (lower left))*

By discovering the Lorentz transformation, Einstein resolved the constancy of the speed of light, and realized that time and space are essentially the same — leading to the understanding that we do not live in time + 3-dimensional space but in **4-dimensional spacetime**.

The following completely unexpected results were also obtained:

- Mass is one form of energy: $E = mc^2$
- Antimatter exists.
- The intrinsic spin of elementary particles such as electrons and positrons exists **because** time and space together constitute 4-dimensional spacetime.
