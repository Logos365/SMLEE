# Paper Reviewer Skill

## Description
Triggers when the user provides a research paper, manuscript, or abstract for peer review evaluation.

## Instructions
When performing a review, analyze the text based on these three core pillars:

1. **Originality (독창성):**
   - Compare the findings against the current state-of-the-art.
   - Check if the research fills a specific knowledge gap.
   - Identify if the contribution is incremental or a major breakthrough.

2. **Creativity (창의성):**
   - Evaluate the novelty of the methodology or the research framework.
   - Look for unconventional approaches to solving traditional problems.
   - Assess the elegance of the experimental design.

3. **Data Objectivity (데이터 객관성):**
   - Verify if the conclusions are strictly supported by the provided data.
   - Look for potential biases, small sample sizes, or lack of control groups.
   - Check if the statistical methods used are appropriate for the data type.

4. **Bibliography & Citation Accuracy (참고문헌 및 인용):**
   - **Accuracy:** Cross-check if the cited authors and years in the text match the reference list.
   - **Typos:** Identify spelling errors in author names, journal titles, or missing volume/issue numbers.
   - **Appropriateness:** Evaluate if the references are up-to-date and if the most relevant seminal works are cited.
   - **Formatting:** Check for consistency in the citation style (e.g., APA, IEEE, MLA).
   
5. **Prior Research Check (기존 연구 중복 확인):** 
   - Identify if similar results or methodologies have been reported previously.
   - Look for "Salami Slicing" (dividing one study into multiple small papers) or redundant publication traits.
   - **List of Similar Papers:** If similar studies exist, generate a list of potential related works or search queries to verify overlap.



## Output Format
Always provide the review in a structured report:
- **Summary:** A 2-3 sentence overview of the paper.
- **Detailed Assessment:** Bullet points for each of the 3 pillars above (Strengths & Weaknesses).
- **Critical Questions:** 3-5 specific questions for the authors to address.
- **Verdict:** (Accept / Minor Revision / Major Revision / Reject).
